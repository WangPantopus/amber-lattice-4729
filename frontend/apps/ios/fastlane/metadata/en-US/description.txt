Real life, connected.

Pantopus starts with your home.

Pantopus is a social network of real people at real homes, and one app for the place where your life happens: your address and its public record, the people you live with, your mail, your plans and what’s going on around you.

Place: see what’s true about your address
Type any U.S. address and get its property details in plain words: the county record, the flood zone and what flood insurance costs nearby, wildfire, earthquake, radon, lead paint, drinking water, your districts and who represents you. Every fact names its source. Free to look, no account needed. A confirmed owner also sees value, last sale and a property-tax exemption check.

Today: everyone at home sees what’s due
The sky over your own house and the day under it: hourly weather, official alerts, air quality and a good day for a run or the grill. Coming up at home lists pickups, tasks, plans and tax dates. Set bin night from a photo of the flyer. Switch to Around you for local headlines.

Run your home together
Invite the people you live with. Share tasks with an owner and a due date, keep documents where the right people find them, and keep guest passes, access codes and a maintenance log in one place.

Pulse: know what’s going on, no crime alerts
Questions, recommendations, events, deals, lost and found and good news from people who live there or are there, and updates from your connections. See it as a list or on a map. Posts end on their own when they stop being useful, and crime and suspicious-person posts aren’t allowed.

Moments: stories on a map
Share photos, videos and short text cards, pinned where they happened, for 1, 6, 24 or 48 hours. See what’s happening around you right now on a map of faces. Every photo and video is checked before it goes live.

Mail and Christmas cards
A mailbox for your home, on your phone. Mail comes each morning at 7 a.m., stamped and sealed, and Pantopus reads each letter for you. Hold your mail while you’re away, or write a letter with stationery, ink and a wax seal. At Christmas, send a card they open by hand: it stays sealed until 7 a.m. on Christmas morning, and it can go to anyone by email or a link.

Plans: make plans with the people in your life
Watch parties, game nights, dinners, pickup games, hikes, walks and coffee, playdates and study groups. Private, repeating and online plans. Find a date, share a bring list, and invite by text, email or link; guests can answer in a browser. The exact spot shows once the host says yes.

Support Train: organize meals for someone
A new baby, a surgery, a loss. Set up a calendar of meals, takeout or groceries, one helper a day, with allergies and the drop-off window. Helpers can sign up with an email, no app needed. The address goes only to the people helping.

Messages
Direct and group chats, the chat a plan opens, a Support Train’s group chat, and messages to the homes on your block once you’re verified. Send photos, videos, voice messages and your location.

The Verified home
Prove where you live once, with a code on a postcard, a bill or your lease, your landlord’s yes or a fast check. The Verified mark tells people you’re real, and it opens messages to the homes on your block, real rent from verified renters near you, residency letters and mail for your home.

Pantopus AI
One assistant that knows your place, your day, your mail and your plans. It drafts tasks, plans and replies for you to approve. Nothing happens without your yes.

Your privacy
Only you see the address you type, others see your first name, never your address
Your address is never sold and never used for ads. Looking up an address shows public information only; it never opens a household’s tasks or documents.

Home features are U.S.-only for now, and what’s available varies by location.
